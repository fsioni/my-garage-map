# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Garage MCP aims to be a welcoming, safe, and constructive project. Participants are expected to:

- communicate with empathy, patience, and respect;
- welcome people regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability;
- give actionable technical feedback and accept it gracefully;
- focus disagreement on ideas and observable behavior;
- protect other people's privacy and personal data;
- take responsibility, apologize, and repair harm when mistakes happen.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing,
personal attacks, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

These expectations apply in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews, and other public interactions where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other material that violates these
standards and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

For a private conduct report, contact the maintainer through a private contact method published
on the [`fsioni` GitHub profile](https://github.com/fsioni). Do not disclose sensitive incident
details in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed in good faith with attention to the safety
and privacy of everyone involved.

## Attribution

This code of conduct is project-specific and informed by common open source community standards.
